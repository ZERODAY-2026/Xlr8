RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Residential Rental Agreement ("Agreement") is made on 1 June 2026 between Mr. Rajesh Sharma ("Landlord"), of 14 Lake View Apartments, Indiranagar, Bengaluru 560038, and Ms. Priya Nair ("Tenant"), of 22 Garden Road, Koramangala, Bengaluru 560034.

The Landlord lets to the Tenant the residential property known as Flat 3B, 14 Lake View Apartments, Indiranagar, Bengaluru 560038 ("the Premises") for a period of eleven months, beginning on 15 June 2026 and ending on 14 May 2027.

The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 28,000 (Rupees Twenty-Eight Thousand Only) on the 5th day of each calendar month, by bank transfer to the account nominated by the Landlord.

As security for the performance of the Tenant's obligations, the Tenant has paid a refundable security deposit of Rs. 56,000 (Rupees Fifty-Six Thousand Only), equal to two months' rent. The deposit shall be returned within 30 days of the end of the tenancy, after deduction of any unpaid rent or damage beyond normal wear and tear.

Electricity, water and gas charges are metered separately and are payable directly by the Tenant. The Tenant shall pay maintenance charges of Rs. 1,500 per month to the building association, in addition to rent.

The Tenant shall keep the Premises in good and clean condition and shall not make structural alterations without the prior written consent of the Landlord.

The Landlord shall be responsible for repairs to the structure, plumbing and electrical wiring of the Premises, save where damage is caused by the negligence of the Tenant.

Either party may end this agreement by giving the other party 60 days' written notice.

The Tenant shall not sublet or assign the whole or any part of the Premises without the prior written consent of the Landlord.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of India, and the courts of Bengaluru shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of it.
